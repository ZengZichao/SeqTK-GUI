Third-party notice: kseq.h and khash.h

bin/windows/seqtk.exe is not built from seqtk.c alone. Upstream seqtk.c does
#include "kseq.h" and #include "khash.h", and the upstream Makefile names both
headers as prerequisites of the seqtk target (`seqtk:seqtk.c khash.h kseq.h`).
They are therefore compiled into the distributed binary as part of it, which
makes this a copy of those two libraries as much as a copy of seqtk - and MIT
binds the notice to *all copies or substantial portions*, including a binary.

Both headers are MIT-licensed by Attractive Chaos. Their license blocks are
reproduced below, each verbatim from the top of its own file. The two copyright
lines are worded differently - khash.h reads "2008, 2009, 2011 by Attractive
Chaos", kseq.h reads "2008, 2009, 2011 Attractive Chaos" without the "by" - and
that difference is preserved rather than normalised, because a verbatim notice
is copied, not rewritten.
